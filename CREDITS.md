# Tutorial photos

Made by `scripts/tutorial-photos/build.sh` in the MoonLight repository; MoonLight downloads them when a tutorial starts.

Every photo here comes from raw.pixls.us (https://raw.pixls.us), where each sample is released into the public domain
under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The JPEGs were rendered from the raws by macOS at
1800 pixels on the long edge, with location (GPS) and maker notes left out. The location was also removed from
mountain-valley.dng; terracotta.cr3 is unchanged.
